# Local property review — 2.3.0-dev.1

23 October 2026. The user authorized the five proposed steps: investigate the 30-building cohort, audit 178 unmatched municipal keys, model access chains, retain property history and measure evidence quality. This phase is local only. Published 2.2.0 is unchanged. Energy, company data and geographic expansion remain deferred.

## Results and actual limits

1. **30-case investigation:** Chrome searched official municipal sources for every frozen cohort parcel. Search queries, timestamps and discovered links are retained in ignored `output/qa/property-document-searches.json`. Every case now has an investigation record and next evidence needed. Three cases have inspected documentary observations (Digione, Beaumont and Fabrizi); only Digione sub114 has exact-key historical access wording. All 30 current access arrangements and site reviews remain unverified. Search hits are research leads, not evidence that a property's access has been confirmed. Numeric terms can occur in unrelated rows of a PDF.
2. **178-key audit:** all 178 distinct complete municipal keys without an exact existing APE-register match were assessed. Results: 171 additional inventory-only reported references; 5 supported by additional documentary references; 2 section differences requiring review. The two alternatives are blank municipal section versus `=` in SIPEE for 1179/259/sub2 and sub3. Neither is automatically merged or counted as a new property. The five documentary keys are 1173/331/sub2, 1175/643/sub113, 1221/149/sub2, 1223/9/sub2 and 1224/546/sub42. An additional 32 public cadastral parcel requests completed without errors; together with 18 cached requests they cover all 50 parcels containing the 178 keys. No exact blank-section key matches were found in those responses. Empty responses do not prove no property or no certificate exists. Group primary addresses remain group attributes, not unit entrances. No new apartment count is inferred.
3. **Access chains:** 2,993 parcels have explicit source/candidate graph edges. Nodes distinguish municipal civic points, BDTRE internal access points, existing buildings, acquired property identities and documentary descriptions of a passage. BDTRE address correspondence and footprint proximity remain candidate edges. A document's statement about access from an entrance hall can be a historical documentary edge to its exact key; it does not establish that a present GIS point is the legally correct entrance. Courtyard outlines, stairs, physical routes and apartment positions are not fabricated. Rejected/out-of-validity links are excluded using the local ledger at build time and current source-link decisions when displaying a property chain. Two descriptions of an entrance hall are source observations, not two newly identified physical halls.
4. **Property history:** 54,321 distinct `(property identity, event kind, source event ID)` observations are indexed across the acquired register, deduplicated across parcel dossiers. Certificate submissions/expiry, inventory references and historical documents remain different event types. Source-only keys absent from the APE register have separate documentary-key timelines. Pending cadastral updates and programme years are explicit, not completed changes. No subdivision, merger or renumbering is established in this phase. The optional ignored `output/property-review/identity-events.json` accepts only evidence-backed curated change assertions: before/after full keys, correct cardinality, inspected source/page/hash, bilingual factual claims and an explicitly known effective date when available. No records are generated from address similarity or certificate renewals. Digione 113 and 114 remain separate with relationship unknown.
5. **Evidence quality:** counts use 24,706 distinct property/group IDs, not the sum of per-parcel records (some groups span parcels). Classification: 1 historically documented access, 0 address-only documentary cases after conflict precedence, 18,775 candidate, 725 conflicting/needing reconciliation, 5,205 unknown. Conflict classification takes precedence over weaker candidate/address evidence; detailed source facets remain visible. These describe the acquired inventory, not district cadastral completeness. No independent current truth pairs are available: accuracy, precision and recall are null, never 0% or an unsupported confidence score. The cohort is purposive and cannot estimate district-wide accuracy.

In Property register, the new **Property verification / Verifica immobili** section provides the cohort findings, source-discovery leads, searchable 178-key audit, documentary-key histories and JSON export. Each property shows its relationship-evidence class, access chain, source timeline and Street View actions. White ArcGIS-style surfaces and IT/EN remain. Existing registry totals remain 24,706 identities /27,137 certificates and published source downloads remain the 2.2 baseline. No push or deployment was performed.

Reproduction order: `fetch_review_parcels.py` (resumable, rate-limited, stops on repeated failures); `prepare_review_observations.py`; `build_property_review.py`; `check_property_review.py`. Source-discovery input is the actual Chrome research log, not generated search snippets. The ordinary source/investigation builders still precede this phase when regenerating the whole project.
