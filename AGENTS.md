# Project working instructions

Read `HANDOFF.md`, `DATA_REVIEW.md`, `V2_ROADMAP.md` and `RELEASES.md` before continuing this project. They preserve the user's goal, preferences, verified state and limits.

- Original goal: cadastral parcel → building → lettered entrance → apartment/property → eventual POD and consumption. Public data now; company measurements later. Pilot San Donato, eventual whole Torino.
- Preserve a white ArcGIS Pro/web visual style and complete IT/EN controls.
- Selecting an entrance opens Street View in the same display, bottom-right, with close/minimize/restore.
- Use Chrome extension browser research when accessing sources through a browser.
- Preserve source provenance and distinguish observations, model outputs and unknowns. Do not invent subalterni, apartment geometry, PODs, measured energy or LoD2/2.5 coverage.
- Treat multi-subalterno certificate energy as one attested-property total unless evidence supports a split.
- Keep raw PDFs/downloads, QA output, credentials and future company data out of public publication artifacts. Use the publication allowlist.
- Preserve the version-one baseline before developing the six agreed version-two priorities. Do not overwrite or force-push published history.
- Run relevant source/calculation checks and browser verification after substantive changes. Document actual verification limits.
